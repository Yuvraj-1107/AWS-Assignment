About Us

KIET was established in 1998 and is located on the Ghaziabad-Meerut Road in Delhi-NCR. The university offers engineering, management, computer applications and pharmacy programmes, with experienced faculty, modern labs and a lively campus for students.

Our Courses

MBA / MCA

Postgraduate programmes in management and computer applications for future managers and developers.

B.Pharm

Pharmacy programme covering drug science, health care and pharmaceutical industry skills.
